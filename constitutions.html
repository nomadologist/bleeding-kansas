⚖️ Three Constitutions

🤔 Why Three?

In March 1855, proslavery candidates won 37 of 39 seats in the . called it the “bogus legislature” and set up their own government in Topeka, giving Kansas two governments.

Each side tried to write the constitution Kansas would enter the under. Free-Staters wrote the Topeka Constitution in 1855, the proslavery Lecompton Constitution followed in 1857, and the Wyandotte Constitution of 1859 finally made Kansas a free state on January 29, 1861.
